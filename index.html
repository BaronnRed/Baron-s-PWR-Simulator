About HBM:NTM PWR Planner

This HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod (NTM) Simulator helps Minecraft players design efficient Pressurized Water Reactors (PWR). Use this tool to calculate heat flux, optimize coolant flow, and predict energy output (HE/t) before building in survival mode. Compatible with HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod version 1.7.10 only. Test different fuel rod configurations, moderator blocks, and coolant types without risking a meltdown in your world.
